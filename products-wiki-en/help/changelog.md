# Changelog

## November 2026

* **Gravity Block Exchange (GBX):** send bundles to GBX builders. See [Submit Bundles](../gravity-block-exchange-gbx/submit-bundles.md). 12 Nov.

## October 2026

* **Token tips and Address Lookup Tables:** tip in WSOL, USDC or USDT as well as SOL, and reference the tip account from an Address Lookup Table in v0 transactions. See [Tips and fees](../send-transactions/tips-and-fees.md#tip-in-wsol-usdc-or-usdt).
* **Decoded shreds in Amsterdam.** 27 Oct.
* **Decoded shreds out of beta,** now for tier-2 shred users. 25 Oct.
* **VIP 1 threshold raised from 3 SOL to 4 SOL** of tips over 7 days. 18 Oct.
* **Bundle usage guidelines:** keep a bundle success rate of around 15–20%. Bundle access may be disabled at very low success rates. 18 Oct.
* **Postpacks now in five regions:** Frankfurt, Amsterdam, New York, Singapore and Tokyo. 16 Oct.

## September 2026

* **London endpoint added** (`lon.gateway.astralane.io`). 29 Sep.
* **Postpacks page added,** with PostpackPay for registering tips. 11 Sep.

## August 2026

* **Decoded shreds (beta):** decoded transactions over gRPC instead of raw shreds. 25 Aug.
* **Ghost transactions:** pass the tip as a parameter instead of inside the transaction. Invited users. 11 Aug.

## July 2026

* **Shreds:** register tips through ShredPay over HTTP, and tip on the closing leg of a trade if you prefer. Frankfurt shred relay also on Cherry Servers for co-location. 8 Jul.

## June 2026

* **Astralane MCP** added. 16 Jun.
* **Shred tier prices raised:** tier-1 from 0.1 to 0.2 SOL and tier-2 from 0.3 to 0.5 SOL of tips over 24 hours. 10 Jun.

## September 2025

* **Old Los Angeles endpoint deprecated** (`45.32.86.58`). It stays up for existing users. 23 Sep.

## August 2025

* **4 new tip wallets** added. 12 Aug.
* **New York endpoint moved** to TeraSwitch, to avoid firewall throttling and for better hardware. Old IP `173.231.40.226` retired. 7 Aug.
